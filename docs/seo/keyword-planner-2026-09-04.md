## SEO questions this data answers

1. **What must be answered?** Function-of-X queries. Nine of the twelve Tier-1 heads are
   literally "<structure> function". The site's `/brain/[slug]` pages must lead with a direct
   function answer in the first 40 words.
2. **Biggest content gap:** the LOBES cluster. "lobes of the brain", "occipital lobe function",
   "frontal lobe function" are each 10K–100K with Low competition, and the site has no lobe
   pages — only sub-region pages. This is the single largest untapped cluster found.
3. **Second gap: vascular.** "circle of willis" (10K–100K, Low) and "blood supply of the brain"
   (1K–10K) have no page, though /quiz/artery-territories exists.
4. **Third gap: tracts.** corticospinal / spinothalamic / dorsal column — all 1K–10K, Low,
   no pages, and /quiz/white-matter-tracts already covers the content.
5. **Commercial signal:** "frontal lobe function" tops out at $17.20 CPC — highest in the
   entire dataset (legal/medical advertisers). High-value traffic.

## Conclusions (2026-09-04)

1. Flagship validated: "cranial nerve mnemonics" 10K–100K @ Low comp — the
   /mnemonics/cranial-nerves push is the highest-value play on the site.
2. "dirty cranial nerve mnemonic" 1K–10K — variants section targets it verbatim.
3. Quiz keywords carry commercial CPCs ($1–6) — test-prep advertisers value this traffic.
4. "brain anatomy quiz" (1K–10K) ≫ "neuroanatomy quiz" (100–1K): keep "brain anatomy"
   as primary phrasing site-wide.
5. App-store demand check: "brain anatomy app"/"neuroanatomy app" = 10–100/mo.
   Direct app-search demand is negligible; the web funnel is the strategy.
