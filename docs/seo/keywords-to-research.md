# Brain Atlas — Seed Keywords to Validate

Paste these into Keyword Surfer (free Chrome ext), Surfer, or Ahrefs.
Record: volume / difficulty / intent / what's ranking. Kill anything where
the SERP is all Quizlet + Kenhub unless it's genuinely long-tail.

**Read the SERP before trusting the number.** Course rule: whatever ranks IS
the intent. If the top 5 are YouTube videos, we're not winning it with a web app.

## What to do with the results

1. Fill in volume + difficulty for each.
2. Keep anything with difficulty under ~20 and any real volume.
3. Pick ONE cluster and build it out fully before starting the next.
4. Every page internally links back to the homepage (the money page).

Do not scatter across all 10 clusters. Course chapter 3: finish one first.
